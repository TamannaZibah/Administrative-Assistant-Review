FIFO (First-In, First-Out)

Always place newly delivered lot boxes at the back of shelves. Pull oldest unexpired dates first.

Par-Level & Reorder Point

Par = Optimum baseline quantity required. Reorder triggered as soon as stock drops to or below threshold.

Cold Chain & Biologics

Vaccines & rapid controls must be refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F). Log twice daily.
